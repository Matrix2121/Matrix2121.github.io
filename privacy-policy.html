English

Introduction

Belot Score (“the App”) is a mobile application that helps you track scores for card games such as Belot and other simple card games. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and your choices.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Developer

  • App name: Belot Score
  • Android package: com.vasil.cardgamesscorer
  • Contact: aquamarinestudioos@gmail.com

Summary

  • Game data is stored only on your device.
  • We do not operate a login system or backend server for your scores.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect data under Google’s policies.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your phone or tablet:

  • Game scores, hands, and match history
  • Player names you enter
  • Your optional display name
  • App settings (theme, language, display preferences)
  • Recently used player names

This data stays on your device unless you delete it or uninstall the App. We do not receive or upload this data to our servers.

Information collected by third parties (advertising)

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. When ads are shown, Google and its partners may collect information such as:

  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App interactions related to ads (for example, ad impressions or clicks)
  • Diagnostic information related to ad delivery

This data is transmitted off your device to Google and may be shared with advertising partners according to Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policies.

You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

How we use information

Data Purpose
Local game data To provide scoring, history, and settings inside the App
Ad-related data (via AdMob) Advertising and ad measurement

Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where applicable under GDPR:

  • Local app data: processed to provide the App functionality you request.
  • Advertising data: processed based on your use of the free, ad-supported App and Google’s role as an independent controller for ad services.

Data sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your locally stored game data with third parties.
  • AdMob / Google may share ad-related data with advertising partners as described in Google’s policies.

Data retention

  • Local app data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the App.
  • Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google’s policies.

Your choices and data deletion

App data on your device

You can delete all locally stored App data by:

  1. Uninstalling Belot Score, or
  2. Android: Settings → Apps → Belot Score → Storage → Clear storage

Advertising choices

You can reset or limit ad personalization on Android via Settings → Google → Ads (options may vary by device and region).

Contact us

For privacy questions or deletion requests related to data we control, email aquamarinestudioos@gmail.com. Note: we cannot delete data held by Google on your behalf; use Google’s tools and settings for ad-related data.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will assist with deletion of any data we control.

Security

Data transmitted for advertising uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Local data is stored in the App’s private storage on your device.

International transfers

If you use the App outside your home country, ad-related data may be processed by Google in countries where Google operates, as described in Google’s privacy documentation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Въведение

Belot Score („Приложението“) е мобилно приложение за проследяване на резултати от карти (напр. белот и други прости картови игри). Тази политика обяснява какви данни се обработват и какви са вашите права.

С използването на Приложението приемате тази политика.

Обобщение

  • Игровите данни се съхраняват само на устройството.
  • Няма регистрация и не качваме резултати на сървър.

  • Не продаваме лични данни.

Данни на устройството

Локално се съхраняват: резултати, раздавания, история, имена на играчи, по избор вашето име, настройки (тема, език, дисплей), наскоро използвани играчи.

Не получаваме тези данни — остават на телефона ви.

Данни от трети страни (реклами)

Google AdMob може да събира: рекламни/устройствени идентификатори, взаимодействие с реклами, диагностични данни. Вижте Политиката за поверителност на Google.

Споделяне

  • Локалните игрови данни не споделяме.
  • AdMob/Google споделят рекламни данни според политиките на Google.
  • Не продаваме лична информация.

Изтриване на данни

  1. Деинсталирайте приложението, или
  2. Настройки → Приложения → Belot Score → Памет → Изчистване на паметта

За реклами: Настройки → Google → Реклами (зависи от устройството).
