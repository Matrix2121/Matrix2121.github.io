Introduction

Belot Score (“the App”) is a mobile application that helps you track scores for card games such as Belot and other simple card games. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is collected, how it is used, with whom it is shared, how long it is kept, and your choices — including how to withdraw consent and request deletion.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Summary

  • Game scores, player names, and settings are stored on your device. We do not operate a login system or a backend server for your scores.
  • The free version may show ads through Google AdMob, which can collect advertising-related data off your device.
  • Optional in-app purchases (including ad-free access, extra features, and subscriptions) are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to confirm what you bought.
  • The App may use Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the App is used (screens, sessions, and similar events). This data is not tied to a name or email we collect.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Paid features do not require you to allow tracking or ads.

Request deletion of your data (Belot Score)

This section is for users of Belot Score by Aqua Marine Studio. The App does not create accounts. Follow these steps to request deletion of data we control.

  1. Email aquamarinestudioos@gmail.com with the subject Belot Score data deletion.
  2. If the request involves a purchase, include your Google Play order ID or the email / Apple ID used for the store. Otherwise write that you want analytics or purchase records deleted.

What we delete (usually within 30 days of a valid request):

  • RevenueCat customer and purchase records we can reasonably identify
  • Firebase / Google Analytics records we can reasonably identify (app-instance / usage data associated with your request)

What is deleted on your device when you uninstall the App or clear its storage:

  • Game scores, hands, history, player names, and settings
  • Local purchase-entitlement cache

We do not keep a copy of your game scores on our servers.

What we cannot delete on your behalf (use their tools; they keep data per their own policies and retention periods):

  • Google AdMob / Google advertising data
  • Apple App Store and Google Play billing history
  • Device backups (iCloud / Google Backup)

Uninstalling the App stops new local and analytics collection from that installation. It does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in Google Play or your Apple ID.

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your phone or tablet:

  • Game scores, hands, and match history
  • Player names you enter
  • Your optional display name
  • App settings (theme, language, display preferences)
  • Recently used player names
  • A local cache of purchase entitlements (for example, whether ads are removed) so the App can work offline

We do not receive this game data on our own servers. Your device’s backup settings (for example iCloud Backup or Google Backup) may copy App data to your Apple or Google account. We do not operate those backups.

Accounts

The App does not require an account with us. There is no developer-operated login, profile, or cloud score account to create or delete. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID or Google account through the store, not to an account we host.

Information collected by third parties (advertising)

Unless you have purchased an ad-free entitlement, the App may use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. When ads are shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as:

  • Device or advertising identifiers (on iOS, the Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA only if you allow tracking; on Android, the advertising ID if available)
  • App interactions related to ads (for example, ad impressions or clicks)
  • Diagnostic information related to ad delivery
  • Approximate location or other signals Google uses for ads, depending on your device and regional settings

This data is transmitted off your device to Google and may be shared with advertising partners according to Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policies. Google acts as an independent controller for this advertising data.

You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Information collected by Firebase / Google Analytics

The App may use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect aggregated usage metrics so we can understand which screens and features people use and improve the App. By default this SDK may collect:

  • An app-instance identifier (a random ID for that installation, not your name or email)
  • Android Advertising ID, if available (you can reset or limit this in device settings)
  • Approximate location derived from a masked IP address (for example city or country), not GPS
  • App lifecycle events such as screen views and sessions
  • In-app purchase events (product ID, name, and price) when a store purchase occurs

We do not set a personal User ID, enable Google Signals, or upload your game scores or player names to Analytics. We do not use Firebase Crashlytics; the App does not send crash-log files to us for this purpose.

This data is transmitted to Google over HTTPS and is used for analytics (how the App is used). Google processes it as our analytics provider. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, if the App wants to use the advertising identifier to deliver personalized ads or measure ads across apps and websites, Apple requires permission through App Tracking Transparency. You may see a system prompt explaining that the identifier is used to deliver ads and measure ad performance.

  • If you tap Allow, Google may use the advertising identifier for personalized ads and measurement.
  • If you tap Ask App Not to Track (or later turn tracking off), the App will not access your advertising identifier for tracking. You can still use the App, including paid features. Ads, if shown, should be non-personalized where Google supports that.

You can change this later in iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also limit Apple’s own ad personalization under Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising. Learn more: Control App Tracking permissions on iPhone.

In-app purchases

The App may offer one-time purchases (for example removing ads or unlocking features) and auto-renewable subscriptions. Payment is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your full payment card number, bank details, or Apple/Google password.

We use RevenueCat to validate receipts, restore purchases, and unlock entitlements on your device. RevenueCat may receive:

  • Purchase history and subscription status
  • An anonymous App User ID generated for the purchase SDK
  • Store transaction / receipt information
  • Device type and operating system

We do not send your game scores or player names to RevenueCat. RevenueCat’s practices are described in RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. RevenueCat processes this purchase data to provide the purchase service.

Деинсталирането спира ново събиране от тази инсталация. То не отменя абонамент — управлявайте абонаменти в Google Play или Apple ID.
